Export Link Docs modules

Module 1

Records

  • Customers and importersValidates the tax ID of each country in the Americas. CUITRUTNITRUCRFCEINand more
  • ProductsWith NCM / HS code, photo, Portuguese and Spanish descriptions and packaging data.
  • Price listsOne or more lists per market or per customer.

Module 2

Documents

  • Pro forma invoiceAutomatic numbering and descriptions translated into Spanish.
  • Commercial invoiceCreated from the approved pro forma, with no retyping.
  • Packing listPackages, net weight, gross weight and m³ calculated from packaging data.
  • PDF and ExcelSend the PDF to the importer and use the Excel file for customs clearance.
  • Order importReads the customer's order from Excel, Word or pasted text.

Module 3

Export pricing

  • Starting pointStart from the Brazilian domestic price or from production cost.
  • Taxes out of the priceRemoves the taxes that don't apply to exports under each Brazilian tax regime: Simples, Presumido or Real. Ready for CBS and IBS from 2027.
  • Export costsSuggested values for inland freight, clearance, terminal and insurance, all editable.
