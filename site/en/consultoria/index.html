Market research report

Before choosing where to export, see what foreign trade records show about your product. We build a report from import and export data for dozens of countries, filtered by your product's NCM / HS code.

What the report covers

  • Market sizeHow much each country imports of your product, by value and volume, and how that has changed in recent years.
  • SeasonalityWhich months concentrate purchases, so you can plan prospecting and shipments.
  • Supplier countriesWhere imports come from today and Brazil's share in each market.
  • Reference priceAverage import price per kilo or per unit by country of origin, to compare with your price by Incoterm.
  • Who buysThe main importers of the product in each destination, with purchase frequency and volume, in countries that publish this data.
  • Who already sellsBrazilian exporters and competitors from other countries already serving those buyers.
  • How cargo arrivesThe transport modes and entry ports or customs offices used most.
  • RecommendationThe markets with the most potential to start with and a list of buyers to prospect.

Fee: US$ 150 per country and per NCM code

This is guidance research based on customs records. Some countries publish importer names and others don't, so the level of detail varies by country.
