What the software solves

The most common problems exporters face

Documents in Excel

A pro forma copied from the last order, at the risk of carrying over the wrong customer or price.

With the Export Link software: automatic numbering and data pulled from your records.

Weight and volume errors

Net weight, gross weight and m³ that don't match between the invoice and the packing list.

With the Export Link software: calculated from each product's packaging data.

Pricing without a method

Export prices built on the domestic price, including taxes that don't apply.

With the Export Link software: pricing by tax regime, transport mode and Incoterm.

ERP too expensive

The ERP's export module costs more than the operation can justify.

With the Export Link software: dedicated software sized to your operation.

Positioning

What Export Link is and what it is not

We give your company the tools and the guidance to export on its own.

Export Link is

  • The provider of the Export Link software.
  • Support to build exporting into your own company.
  • Guidance on customs regulations and compliance.

Export Link is not

  • A sales agent: we don't sell on your company's behalf.
  • A customs broker: we don't clear goods.
  • A logistics company: we don't move cargo.
